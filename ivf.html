IVF — In Vitro Fertilisation

Our flagship treatment — eggs and sperm are fertilised in a specialised lab, and healthy embryos are transferred to the uterus. High-tech, carefully monitored, personalised protocols.

Most Common

IUI — Intrauterine Insemination

A less invasive first-line treatment where washed sperm is directly placed into the uterus during ovulation — ideal for mild fertility challenges.

Minimally Invasive

ICSI — Sperm Injection

A single healthy sperm is directly injected into an egg — the gold standard treatment for severe male factor infertility or repeated IVF failures.

How It Works

Your IVF Journey — Step by Step

We guide you through every stage with clarity, care, and complete support.

🩺 1

Initial Consultation

Complete medical history, couple evaluation, and personalised treatment planning.

💊 2

Ovarian Stimulation

Hormone injections to stimulate multiple egg development, monitored by ultrasound.

🔬 3

Egg Retrieval & Fertilisation

Eggs collected under sedation, fertilised with sperm in our IVF laboratory.

🧬 4

Embryo Culture

Fertilised embryos are cultured for 3–5 days. Best quality embryos are selected.

🌱 5

Embryo Transfer & Follow-up

Embryo transferred to the uterus. Pregnancy test after 14 days. Complete support throughout.

Common Questions

IVF — Frequently Asked Questions

What is IVF and how does it work? ▾
IVF (In Vitro Fertilisation) involves stimulating the ovaries to produce multiple eggs, retrieving them, fertilising with sperm in our laboratory, and transferring the best embryo(s) into the uterus. The entire process takes about 2–3 weeks per cycle.
How many IVF cycles will I need? ▾
Many couples achieve success in 1–2 cycles. However, this depends on your age, diagnosis, and response to treatment. Our doctors will discuss realistic expectations during your consultation based on your individual case.
Is IVF painful? ▾
The stimulation injections cause mild discomfort at most. The egg retrieval is done under light sedation so you won't feel pain during the procedure. Some women experience mild cramping afterwards, which resolves quickly.
What is the cost of IVF at Annai Velankanni? ▾
We offer transparent, package-based pricing with no hidden costs. The exact cost depends on the treatment protocol chosen. Please contact us or book a consultation for a detailed breakdown. EMI options are available.
At what age should I consider IVF? ▾
We recommend consulting a fertility specialist if you are under 35 and have been trying for 12 months without success, or over 35 and trying for 6 months. Earlier consultation leads to better outcomes.
Is the consultation confidential? ▾
Absolutely. All consultations, medical records, and treatment details are completely confidential. We understand the sensitivity of fertility treatment and take your privacy very seriously.
