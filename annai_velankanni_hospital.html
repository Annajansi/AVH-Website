Who We Are

Compassionate Care,
World-Class Medicine

Named after the beloved Our Lady of Velankanni, our hospital has been a beacon of hope and healing for the people of Tirunelveli and Southern Tamil Nadu. We combine the latest in medical technology with genuine human care.

Meeting the highest standards of hospital care, patient safety, and quality treatment protocols.
